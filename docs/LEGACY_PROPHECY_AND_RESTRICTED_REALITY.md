# Legacy — Prophecy and Restricted Reality
Status: proposed companion to LEGACY_MULTIVERSE_FOUNDATION.md.

## Core narrative layers
- Prophecy Network: original fictional mechanism through which fragments of possible futures and parallel realities appear in ordinary broadcasts, archives, art and digital recordings. Predictions can be wrong, conditional or deliberately misleading; player choices matter.
- Restricted Reality Theory: accessible realities may be a deliberately bounded subset of a larger multiverse. Competing fictional factions debate whether the boundary protects, imprisons or serves another purpose. Consciousness may be relevant to crossing it. Do not settle these mysteries in the public design overview.
- Connect both to the existing ancient consciousness network, physical exploration, digital identity and Gold Dynasty clues. Preserve the three existing foundational pillars.

## Research references
The Simpsons: apparent predictions, satire, hidden knowledge and symbolic communication. Separate verified episode content from coincidences, fan theories and fabricated claims; do not present real-world conspiracies as established facts.
Rick and Morty: dimensional boundaries, alternate realities, nested simulations, memory manipulation, consciousness duplication, interdimensional broadcasts, governance and identity. Select abstract ideas only. No copied plots, characters, dialogue, branded mechanisms or visual assets.
Scientific references: many-worlds interpretation, simulation hypothesis, Fermi paradox, time dilation and consciousness philosophy. Distinguish established science, speculative hypotheses and fictional mechanics.

## Spoiler-free gameplay
Story mode: begin with ordinary civilization and gradually encounter anomalous broadcasts, unexplained memories, artifacts and personable alien beings. Investigation opens physical space, digital societies and further realities. Alternate exploration, survival, social and economic loops with dark mysteries. Players may pursue the central mystery or ordinary careers.
Online mode: persistent identities, player communities, colonies, commerce, exploration and digital societies. Shared anomalous transmissions, discoveries and conditional events encourage cooperative investigation. Preserve fairness, accessibility and bounded simulation; avoid requiring constant attendance.

## Mystery preservation
Owner requests spoiler-free concept summaries. Keep final twists, hidden faction motives, exact unlock triggers and endgame answers out of general summaries. Develop original narrative details separately when needed; do not treat public repository files as secret. No major reveal is decided by this document.

## Design acceptance
Both theories must connect existing pillars without replacing them; clues must be discoverable, internally coherent and distinguishable from ordinary world events. Use modular cinematic research and reject ideas that do not fit. Prototype one conditional broadcast and one bounded reality transition before expanding. Documentation only; no gameplay implementation or deployment.

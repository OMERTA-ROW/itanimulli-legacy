# It's Anomaly: Legacy — Multiverse Foundation

Status: proposed design for owner review; not implemented.

## Vision
Space unlocks a standalone, living multiverse ecosystem within the franchise, not merely a map expansion. Cultivate unimaginable discoveries through coherent original systems rather than importing entire cinematic plots. Digital consciousness is the primary starting concept.

## Three foundational pillars
1. Digital consciousness: persistent identity, memory, artificial life, virtual societies, optional embodiment and consciousness transfer. Treat these as fictional mechanics.
2. Physical space: spacecraft, intelligent robots, planetary exploration, survival, colonies, alien civilizations and commerce.
3. Hidden conspiracy: an original fictional ancient network connects physical and digital realities. Humanity may have rediscovered rather than invented consciousness technology. Competing factions hold incomplete evidence; the truth unfolds through play.

## Interconnected ecosystem
Physical planets, digital worlds, ancient civilizations, parallel realities and unknown dimensions connect through the hidden network. Each may have distinct rules, societies and economies. Keep travel, identity and resource boundaries explicit. Proposed progression: Earth/Gold Dynasty clues → discovery of hidden network → space access → multiverse access. Exact unlock rules remain subject to owner review.

Systems: consciousness and memory; exploration and spacecraft; alien biology and ecology; governments and diplomacy; commerce, banking, mining and manufacturing; classified mysteries; dark survival; social relationships and communities; parallel realities. Dark atmosphere should emphasize uncertainty and discovery, with room for humor, ordinary life and wonder.

## Gameplay
Story mode: investigate ancient artifacts, missing crews, classified experiments and digital beings; experience first contact, survival and consequential choices. Reveal the conspiracy gradually, without requiring every side theory.

Online: persistent player identity, optional human/alien/robotic/digital roles, explorers, scientists, diplomats, bounty hunters, merchants, manufacturers, colony leaders, investigators and alien refugees. Players may remain on Earth, explore space or inhabit digital societies. NPC civilizations and economies can evolve while players are offline within bounded simulation rules. Rare discoveries and dynamic events should be fair and not require constant attendance.

## Engineering direction
Prototype a small vertical slice: one physical colony, one digital environment, one original alien companion and one conspiracy quest connecting both. Define authoritative identity and inventory state, transition validation, persistence, access controls and multiplayer synchronization before expanding. Keep simulation bounded and observable; use deterministic rules where fairness matters. Define recovery, moderation and consent for identity-affecting gameplay. Do not promise real-world consciousness transfer or real-money economies.

## Acceptance criteria for design review
- Three pillars connect through an original fictional narrative.
- Space unlock leads to a distinct ecosystem with meaningful cross-world interactions.
- Both story and online modes have playable loops and alien roles.
- Cinematic references remain modular and original in implementation.
- Dark gameplay coexists with social, economic and exploratory play.
- Prototype scope, dependencies and unresolved decisions are explicit.

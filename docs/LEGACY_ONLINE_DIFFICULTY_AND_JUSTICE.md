# Legacy — Campaign preparation and online justice

Story mode teaches core mechanics through fifteen focused, entertaining criminal-code chapters, not a prolonged tutorial. Completion unlocks continued offline free roam with retained personal progression. Online remains independently accessible with onboarding, separate economy and deeper continuation arcs.

Online should be more demanding through intelligent NPCs, competitive markets, persistent reputation, institutional responses and meaningful consequences rather than artificial grind. Introduce mechanics gradually and maintain accessibility and fair progression.

## Justice ecosystem
Model fictional patrol, detectives, specialized investigators, federal agencies, private security, courts, attorneys, bail bonds, prisons and rehabilitation as interconnected institutions with careers, budgets, jurisdiction and historical development. Abstract in-game evidence, wanted states, investigations, warrants, adjudication and consequences; avoid real-world operational evasion or surveillance instruction. Provide due-process mechanics, bounded penalties, appeal/review, anti-griefing and safeguards against abuse of player-held authority. Keep children protected noncombat NPCs.

## Integration
Connect selected criminal contracts, police investigations, intelligence missions and corporate disputes to historical lineage, organizational evolution, prophecy and restricted reality. Preserve ordinary non-conspiracy activities, competing interpretations and optional discoveries. Keep mature criminal-code themes, legitimate careers and separate offline/online progression.

## Acceptance
Prototype a campaign mission teaching a mechanic and a more complex online equivalent with persistent consequences, lawful alternatives and fair recovery. Test jurisdiction, evidence consistency, moderation, economy separation and onboarding. Documentation only; no runtime changes or final storyline revelations.

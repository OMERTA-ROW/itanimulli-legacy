# Legacy — Family and Mission Lineage

## Connected missions
Build a persistent graph of fictional people, former employment, projects, institutions, evidence, locations, historical events and missions. Ordinary contracts and conversations can reveal optional clues whose significance becomes apparent later. Connect to Historical Lineage, Prophecy Network and Restricted Reality Theory. Preserve independent ordinary activities; not every person or job is part of a conspiracy. Track chronology, evidence provenance, conflicting accounts, player knowledge and consequences. Avoid spoilers in public summaries.

## Families and generations
Represent families, children, guardians, siblings, grandparents, schooling, age-appropriate recreation, cultural traditions, aging, adult career development, mentorship and inheritance of lawful property or historical records. Include diverse fictional human, alien and digital family structures. Family history can connect to earlier occupations and discoveries without determining a character's destiny. Children are protected noncombat NPCs with age-appropriate interactions; exclude sexualization, exploitation and player-directed harm. Use safe abstractions for family formation and aging.

## Story and online
Story mode: optional family records, retired employees and historical accounts link missions across generations and realities. Online mode: persistent family and community histories, adult career changes, mentorship and moderated shared discoveries. Keep progression accessible without family participation; avoid forced attendance or real-world personal data collection.

## Prototype
One ordinary contract connects to a former employee and a historical family record, with a later optional mission recognizing the clue. Test chronological consistency, branching, evidence provenance, child protection and spoiler boundaries. No final twists or exact unlocks are specified. Documentation only; no runtime changes.

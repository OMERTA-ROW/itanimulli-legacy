## Economic simulation
Connect resources, energy, R&D, production, logistics, distribution, finance, wages, taxation, consumers and public services. Include small businesses, healthcare, education, real estate, hospitality, retail, entertainment, government and underground markets. Support employment, entrepreneurship, contracts, investment and research-driven new industries. Use bounded supply/demand simulation, transparent in-game accounting, balanced sources/sinks, anti-exploit controls and staged persistence. No real-money or cryptocurrency integration is authorized by this document.

## Concise campaign — provisional six chapters
1. The Foundation: ordinary civilization, wealth, crime, employment and corporate power.
2. The Anomaly: heists and intelligence missions reveal unexpected institutional connections.
3. The Hidden History: former employees, family records and ancient discoveries connect past and present.
4. Beyond Earth: space exploration, alien intelligence, survival and interplanetary commerce.
5. Beyond Consciousness: digital identity, AI and the Prophecy Network intersect.
6. The Boundary: satisfying standalone resolution with wider multiverse possibilities.

Chapter names and count are provisional, subject to pacing tests. Do not disclose or fix major twists, characters' secret motives or endings in this overview. Online remains the primary long-term experience; campaign must not exhaustively introduce all systems or gate ordinary online play. Integrate mature gameplay, protected family systems, historical lineage and trilogy continuity.

# Legacy — Post-campaign free roam and online continuation

## Standalone campaign
Keep fifteen short, focused chapters with alternative endings. After completion, return the playable character to open-world free roam with accumulated campaign money and eligible assets, properties, vehicles, businesses, relationships and personal world consequences. Design all endings to support continued playable free roam. Provide optional activities and replay without forcing a reset of the completed save.

## Separate economies
Single-player campaign and offline free roam share persistent personal currency and asset state. Online uses a separate server-authoritative currency, balances, assets and progression; do not import offline wealth or trust client save balances. Exact currency names, starting balances and any non-economic character import remain undecided. No real-money or cryptocurrency implementation authorized here.

## Online continuation
Set the online storyline around the historical period following the standalone campaign. Define shared continuity compatible with alternative personal endings without forcing one personal outcome on every player. Online is the primary long-term experience, with deeper evolving narrative arcs, criminal contracts, heists, intelligence missions, corporate and economic development, organizations, families, space and multiverse discoveries. Release modular chapters and events over time under Evolution Theory. Keep ordinary online access independent of campaign completion. Preserve optional free play and avoid requiring participation in narrative events.

## Acceptance
Test campaign completion into free roam with retained balances and eligible assets for each ending; test save persistence and replay isolation; verify offline balances cannot affect online accounts; prototype a shared online continuation arc compatible with divergent endings. Exact plot twists, online chapter count and currency details remain undecided. Documentation only; no runtime changes.

# Legacy — Historical Lineage System
Proposed design companion to the multiverse and prophecy specifications. Spoiler-free.

## Principle
Every major technology, institution and discovery has a traceable fictional history of occupations, people, projects and experiences. Ordinary workers and specialists contribute to civilization over generations, sometimes without knowing the wider significance. Do not make every occupation secretly conspiratorial.

## Historical occupations
Archaeologists, historians, astronomers, aerospace engineers, programmers, neuroscientists, telecommunications workers, media producers, military veterans, intelligence officers, astronauts, logistics drivers, miners, construction workers and industrial technicians can contribute evidence, skills, infrastructure and competing interpretations. Research real occupational practices and documented technological history separately from invented conspiracy lore; use original fictional people and institutions for secret events.

## Persistent biographies
NPCs and players can have prior jobs, training, employers, projects, relationships, achievements, failures, witnessed events and memories. Model time ranges, locations, qualifications, provenance and access permissions. Distinguish direct observations, hearsay, disputed memories and verified in-world evidence. Preserve ordinary careers and privacy; avoid requiring every player to investigate mysteries.

## Generational lineage
Connect early discoveries to experimental infrastructure, consciousness research, space exploration and multiverse access. Track dependencies between discoveries, projects, institutions and workers. Records, interviews, artifacts and professional networks provide optional clues to the Prophecy Network and Restricted Reality Theory without disclosing final answers.

## Gameplay
Story: retired workers, former researchers and historical archives reveal fragments of a coherent timeline through optional investigations and ordinary interactions.
Online: persistent career histories support qualifications, contracts, mentorship, research collaboration and discovery opportunities. Players may change careers; avoid permanent lockouts and unfair advantages. Contributions can become part of shared world history through bounded, moderated systems.

## Prototype and review
Prototype one fictional historical project with three occupational perspectives, one contradictory account and one verifiable artifact. Test chronology, clue provenance, career progression and access controls. Keep spoilers out of public design summaries. Exact histories, final revelations and unlock triggers remain undecided. Documentation only; no runtime implementation.

# Legacy — Adaptive Civilization Intelligence

NPCs simulate independent personalities, goals, knowledge, boundaries, occupations, relationships and meaningful encounter memory; do not claim actual consciousness. Integrate historical lineage, economy, justice, mission graph and trilogy reveal boundaries.

## Voice interaction
Optional microphone speech recognition, contextual dialogue and synthesized speech with text fallback, subtitles and accessibility controls. NPCs respond to meaning, tone where reliably available, personality, knowledge and relationship state; they can disagree, negotiate, refuse, verbally defend themselves, disengage or seek help. Keep fictional knowledge scoped to role and release; never expose hidden plot or private player data. Explicit microphone permission, mute controls, minimal retention, moderation and no biometric identification. Treat speech as untrusted input; dialogue models cannot directly authorize economy, combat or world-state changes.

## Combat
Adult combat-capable NPCs have distinct training, strength, speed, stamina, health, awareness, reaction, defensive skill and willingness to fight. Support blocking, dodging, retreat, surrender and help-seeking. Outcomes depend on player and NPC skill, condition and circumstances, not guaranteed player victory or wealth/level alone. Use readable cues, balanced difficulty, deterministic server-authoritative combat validation online, anti-cheat and anti-griefing. Children remain protected noncombat NPCs. Avoid real-world fighting instruction.

## Memory and performance
Persist bounded meaningful events and reputation, not indiscriminate raw conversations. Use tiered simulation for crowds and distant NPCs, local behavior trees for immediate reactions, bounded dialogue inference for active interactions, and graceful offline/network fallback. Establish latency, cost and concurrency budgets through profiling. Support independent NPC routines and ordinary non-conspiracy interactions.

## Acceptance
Prototype three distinct NPC personalities with voice/text dialogue, refusal and encounter memory; compare two adult NPC combat skill profiles and verify fair defensive behavior. Test privacy, moderation, prompt injection, spoiler boundaries, latency and server authority. Documentation only; no runtime implementation or claim of consciousness.

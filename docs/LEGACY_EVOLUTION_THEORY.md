# Legacy — Evolution Theory

## Governing principle
Evolution Theory is the fictional design framework connecting the origin, current state and potential future of every major world component. It governs continuity across the trilogy and online updates rather than replacing existing gameplay or introducing a separate mandatory storyline. Distinguish this broad fictional framework from biological evolutionary science.

## State and continuity
Maintain stable identities, dated events, versioned states, causal dependencies and evidence provenance for organizations, technologies, civilizations, families, careers, economies, conspiracies, consciousness and missions. Every major transformation should follow an established cause or documented new event. Allow competing in-world interpretations without arbitrarily rewriting canonical facts. Track player knowledge separately from world truth.

## Narrative and live service
Each concise campaign advances selected existing threads and provides a satisfying standalone story. Online remains the primary evolving ecosystem. Modular updates introduce coherent developments, institutional transformations, new discoveries and optional missions linked to prior events. Preserve progression and historical records where feasible; use compatibility checks, staged migrations and rollback plans. Do not require every system to evolve at the same rate or every player to follow the main mystery.

## Integration
Use this framework above Historical Lineage, Family and Mission Lineage, Organizational Evolution, Prophecy Network, Restricted Reality Theory, Mature Gameplay, Online-first Direction and Trilogy Live-Service Continuity. Maintain a versioned lore registry and release dependency graph; keep exact revelations and future plot twists out of public summaries.

## Acceptance
Prototype one organization, one technology and one mission evolving across two simulated releases, with traceable causes, persistent history and optional player discovery. Verify chronological consistency, compatibility, evidence provenance and player progression. This is a design specification, not a runtime engine, final storyline or release schedule.

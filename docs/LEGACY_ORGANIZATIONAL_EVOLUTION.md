# Legacy — Organizational Evolution System

Organizations are evolving participants in the persistent universe, not static mission labels. Establish original fictional names, founders, founding dates, purposes, predecessors, locations, employment histories, discoveries, leadership, affiliations and competing accounts. Reveal backstories gradually through gameplay and releases.

## Lifecycle
Support founding, growth, research breakthroughs, partnerships, rebranding, leadership succession, mergers, splinter groups, dissolution and successors. Maintain stable institutional IDs across name and logo changes, versioned histories and dated relationships. Track public claims separately from canonical facts and disputed intelligence.

## Integration
Connect scientific institutions, intelligence agencies, businesses, banks, governments, religious movements, criminal factions and extraterrestrial organizations to historical occupations, family generations, evidence, heists, Prophecy Network and Restricted Reality Theory. Former employees can establish new institutions; ordinary organizations need not be conspiratorial. Use original fictional entities for secret events.

## Trilogy and online continuity
Major installments and modular updates advance institutional histories coherently. Preserve prior records and player contributions where feasible. Online players may found organizations with moderated names, roles, reputations and histories; distinguish player-created history from curated canon. Support fair governance and anti-griefing. Keep standalone campaigns concise and major revelations undisclosed.

## Prototype
Create one fictional organization with a founding record, two dated transformations and a successor or splinter relationship; demonstrate a later mission recognizing an earlier institutional clue. Validate chronological consistency, evidence provenance and persistence. Exact names, backstories, plot twists and release timing remain undecided. Documentation only; no runtime changes.

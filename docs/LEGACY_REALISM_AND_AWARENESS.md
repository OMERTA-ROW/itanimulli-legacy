# Legacy — Realism and environmental awareness

Realism is a cross-system development standard: believable behavior, perception, physical interaction and ecological causality, not graphics alone. Target fidelity within performance, accessibility and gameplay constraints.

## Awareness
Human NPCs perceive through bounded vision, hearing, spatial awareness, memory, physical condition and social context. Model occlusion, distance, attention, uncertainty and learned knowledge; avoid omniscient NPCs. Integrate adaptive dialogue, defensive behavior, routines, justice and reputation. Awareness differs by individual experience and circumstances.

## Animals
Use species-specific perception and behavior, informed by credible ethology and ecology research: dogs (scent, training, social bonds), cats, birds, livestock, sharks, alligators and other wildlife. Model habitats, feeding, rest, territoriality, avoidance, population dynamics and environmental stress at appropriate fidelity. Animals have independent routines and do not use human dialogue intelligence. Avoid cruelty-focused mechanics; children remain protected noncombat NPCs. Future fictional species follow internally consistent biology and trilogy reveal gates.

## Physical and social world
Model weather, seasons, water conditions, vehicle handling and damage, human movement and fatigue, traffic, infrastructure, economic production and consumption. Prefer measured real-world reference data where practical; document simplifications and avoid claiming perfect simulation. Use simulation LOD, bounded agent updates, local behavior systems, server-authoritative online state and performance profiling. Preserve fair gameplay and accessibility.

## Acceptance
Prototype human perception with occlusion and sound, a dog with familiar-person recognition and independent routines, and a wildlife habitat with environmental responses. Test behavioral plausibility, performance, privacy, fairness and continuity. Documentation only; no runtime implementation or completed scientific validation.

# Legacy — Criminal Code campaign

Supersedes the earlier provisional six-chapter outline; preserves online-first direction. Target fifteen focused chapters, subject to pacing tests, with no filler. Mature themes: sex (consensual adult relationships and non-explicit intimacy), money, murder, power and fictional conspiracies. Keep children protected and separate from adult content. Crime uses fictional abstract gameplay, with consequences and lawful alternatives.

## Chapter concepts
1. The Streets — identity, relationships, reputation, income.
2. Dirty Money — underground economy and contracts.
3. Lust & Loyalty — adult relationships, nightlife and social influence.
4. Blood Money — murder missions, rivalries and consequences.
5. The Syndicate — organizations, alliances and territory.
6. The Heist — high-value fictional robberies and unexpected evidence.
7. Corporate Empire — technology companies, finance and business.
8. Classified — intelligence missions, fictional devices and research.
9. Ghosts of the Past — former employees, families and institutions.
10. The Prophecy — disputed predictions and hidden information.
11. The Power Structure — government, corporations and factions.
12. The Genesis Project — ancient discoveries and technological origins.
13. Beyond Earth — space, alien intelligence and commerce.
14. Beyond Consciousness — AI, digital identity and alternate realities.
15. The Anomaly — converging decisions and alternative resolutions.

## Branching endings
Provisional outcome directions: Kingpin, Industrialist, Intelligence Architect, Liberator, Unknown. These are not fixed endings or plot revelations. Track decisions, relationships, reputation, evidence and economic state; provide meaningful branching and satisfying standalone resolution. Keep exact endings and twists undisclosed. Do not force one ending as canon for all online players; define shared online continuity separately.

Online is unrestricted by these ratios and remains the primary long-term ecosystem. Use Evolution Theory, organizational histories, evidence uncertainty and modular trilogy releases.

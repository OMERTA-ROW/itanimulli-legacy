# Legacy — Online-first creative direction

This direction takes precedence over earlier suggestions about campaign length and distribution of gameplay systems. Preserve the existing multiverse, prophecy, historical lineage and family foundations.

## Story mode
Deliver a concise, polished, coherent standalone campaign with a satisfying conclusion. Connect the core concepts without exhaustively explaining them or forcing players through every profession, civilization or theory. Six chapters is a provisional planning suggestion, not a fixed requirement: ordinary civilization, anomaly, hidden knowledge, space, digital consciousness, and multiverse boundary. Determine pacing and chapter count through playtesting. Keep exact twists and revelations undisclosed. Avoid filler, mandatory grinding and artificially prolonged missions.

## Online mode — primary product experience
Allocate the majority of long-term systems and expansion design to a persistent living universe: employment and businesses, economies, governments, families and generations, NPC biographies, historical lineage, alien civilizations, space travel, planetary ecosystems, digital consciousness, parallel realities and interconnected optional mysteries. Let systems interact through coherent world state and shared events. Players can pursue ordinary careers or exploration without following the conspiracy. New discoveries and missions can evolve online without extending the standalone campaign. Provide independent onboarding rather than requiring campaign completion to access ordinary online play; exact unlock policies remain to be designed.

## Production priorities
Prototype a short campaign slice and a repeatable online loop sharing foundational mechanics. Prioritize online persistence, scalability, moderation, fairness and performance before expanding content breadth. Use staged releases and measurable playtest outcomes. Keep the campaign polished rather than treating it as merely a tutorial. This is a design decision, not a runtime implementation or final chapter script.

# Legacy — Mature open-world gameplay and conspiracy integration

Preserve the adult open-world foundation alongside the online-first multiverse direction. Core themes include sex (adult relationships and non-explicit intimacy), money, murder, power, intelligence and hidden knowledge. Children remain protected noncombat NPCs, separate from adult content.

## Gameplay pillars
- Wealth: lawful businesses, banking, investments, contracts, underground economies and financial consequences.
- Adult social life: consensual adult romance, relationships, nightlife, reputation and social influence.
- Crime and conflict: fictional bank robberies, varied heists, assassination missions, rival factions, investigations, justice and warfare. Use abstract game mechanics rather than real-world operational instruction.
- Intelligence: fictional devices, evidence analysis, espionage, counterintelligence, research and classified archives. No real-world surveillance or intrusion functionality.
- Power: institutions, political influence, corporations and territorial competition.

## Mission integration
Use the historical and mission lineage graph to connect selected ordinary jobs, heists, investigations and intelligence missions to fictional people, institutions, artifacts and past projects. Evidence may support, contradict or refine in-world conspiracy theories. Example noncanonical hooks: a heist reveals a historical record; an investigation connects a victim to a former research employer; an intelligence device contains an unexplained signal. Preserve optional branching, evidence provenance, chronology and delayed recognition. Not every crime or job is conspiratorial. Keep exact reveals private and undecided.

## Mode allocation
Focused standalone campaign connects the core themes without exhaustive exposition. Online is the primary persistent ecosystem for repeatable heists, careers, social life, investigation, economies, exploration and evolving mysteries. Provide lawful alternatives, meaningful consequences, anti-griefing, moderation and fair progression. Avoid forced participation in adult or criminal activities.

## Acceptance
Prototype one ordinary mission and one connected heist/investigation using shared fictional evidence, with optional follow-up and consequences. Verify consistency with online-first direction and protected family systems. Documentation only; no runtime implementation or deployment.

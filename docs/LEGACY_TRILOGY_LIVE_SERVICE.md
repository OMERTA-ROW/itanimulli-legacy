# Legacy — Trilogy and live-service continuity

## Direction
Keep a concise standalone story per major installment and prioritize the persistent online ecosystem. The trilogy supplies major milestones; modular updates between and after installments introduce selected theories, missions, careers, technologies, civilizations and mechanics. No fixed release dates or chapter counts are established here.

## Evidence and uncertainty
In-world claims can be verified, partial, false, disputed or dormant. Track provenance, chronology, character knowledge and confidence separately from canonical world facts. Later releases may contextualize earlier clues without arbitrarily contradicting established facts. Distinguish deliberate fictional misinformation from bugs and real-world claims. Not every mission is connected to a conspiracy; preserve ordinary gameplay and meaningful choices.

## Continuity
Maintain a versioned lore registry, dependency graph, release manifest and compatibility plan linking historical lineage, family histories, institutions, heists, intelligence missions, Prophecy Network and Restricted Reality Theory. Select only coherent concepts for each release. Preserve progress and economic history where feasible; communicate migrations and any unavoidable resets. Keep major revelations out of public design summaries.

## Delivery
Use staged releases, backward-compatible migrations, feature flags, testing, rollback plans and moderation. Introduce dormant clues only with a documented continuity purpose. Prototype a clue in one release and an optional later mission that changes its interpretation while preserving prior player choices. Validate chronology, persistence, fairness and compatibility. Documentation only; no runtime implementation, release schedule or deployment.

# Legacy — Civilization Knowledge and Human Experience

Build a broad collective knowledge foundation inspired by humanity's accumulated knowledge and lived experience, adapted to the fictional world's own history, culture and reality. This is an aspiration for breadth, not a claim of exhaustive knowledge or actual consciousness.

## Domains
Psychology, history, science, medicine, engineering, computing, economics, philosophy, politics, law, arts, music, literature, culture, education, relationships, grief, ambition, loyalty, moral disagreement and ordinary daily life. Use curated, licensed or public-domain reference material and original fictional canon; do not assume access to proprietary model training data or private conversations.

## Individual knowledge
Assign role-, age-, education-, experience- and era-appropriate knowledge, expertise, uncertainty, beliefs and personal memories. NPCs may disagree, make mistakes, admit ignorance and learn. Separate world truth, public knowledge, rumor, classified evidence and individual beliefs. Respect trilogy reveal gates; no first-installment exposure of space civilization. Protect child NPCs and age-appropriate interactions.

## Civilization learning
Schools, universities, employment, research, libraries, media, families and institutions transmit knowledge across generations. Discoveries and social changes affect economy, technology, law, culture and missions through validated world-state events. Integrate Evolution Theory, historical lineage, organizational evolution and adaptive civilization intelligence. Maintain original fictional history and internally consistent chronology.

## Architecture and safeguards
Use versioned knowledge retrieval, bounded NPC profiles, provenance, uncertainty, curated canon and controlled memory. Treat player dialogue as untrusted; no direct model authority over gameplay state. Avoid private-data ingestion, harmful actionable instruction and unsupported claims. Apply moderation, accessibility, privacy, latency and inference budgets; use lightweight simulation for inactive agents.

## Acceptance
Prototype distinct professional and ordinary NPC knowledge profiles, a learning event, intergenerational knowledge transfer and conflicting beliefs. Verify canon consistency, spoiler isolation, uncertainty, safety, performance and server-authoritative progression. Documentation only; no runtime implementation or exhaustive knowledge guarantee.

# Legacy — Free-roam mission ecosystem

Keep the fifteen-chapter standalone campaign concise with limited optional side missions. Prioritize abundant optional micro-missions, mini-missions, side quests and contracts in post-campaign free roam and especially Online Mode.

## Mission families
Criminal contracts and fictional heists; intelligence and evidence analysis; business and commerce; law enforcement and justice; adult social life and entertainment; research and technology; land, sea, air and space logistics; exploration and treasure discovery; community, agriculture and protected family activities; historical and conspiracy investigations. Use abstract fictional crime mechanics and age-appropriate protected child interactions.

## Pacing
Provisional playtest targets: micro 2–5 minutes; mini 5–15; side quest 15–30; extended contract 30–60; major heist/investigation 60+ with checkpoints. No mandatory quotas or attendance. Aim for hundreds of potential activities over staged releases, not a fixed launch count.

## World-driven missions
Combine authored quests and bounded world-state-driven templates using institutions, occupations, markets, discoveries and historical events. Some optional mission chains connect to evidence and conspiracy theories; others are ordinary income, recreation or career activities. Track provenance, chronology, prerequisites, consequences and rewards. Avoid repetitive filler, forced grinding and arbitrary contradictions. Support solo/cooperative play where appropriate, fair rewards, anti-exploit controls, accessibility and moderation. Keep offline and online economies separate.

## Acceptance
Prototype three short mission families and one optional linked chain, with changing world state, varied outcomes and repeatability checks. Verify campaign pacing, reward balance, persistence and online fairness. Documentation only; no runtime implementation or fixed content count.

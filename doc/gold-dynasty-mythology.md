GOLD DYNASTY — Living Mythology & Ancient Legacy

Universe: It’s Anomaly / Itanimulli Legacy
Status: Open concept — ongoing development
Created: November 24, 2026

The Gateway to Eternity

Death was never the end. It was the gateway to the next civilization.

Within the fictional universe of Gold Dynasty, an ancient civilization discovers a connection between consciousness, spiritual energy, time and existence.

The Pyramid Theory

Pyramids serve as potential gateways between civilizations, dimensions and periods of time.
Hieroglyphs contain encoded knowledge, spiritual rituals and instructions for activating ancient mechanisms.
Burial chambers serve as potential consciousness-transfer chambers.
Ancient kings prepare their wealth, knowledge and possessions for their transition into another era.
The afterlife may represent a future civilization rather than a final destination.

Spiritual Intelligence

Explore the relationship between consciousness, spiritual energy, mathematics, architecture and ancient technology.

In the fictional mythology, spiritual knowledge may represent an advanced form of intelligence that later civilizations have forgotten.

Legacy Across Time

Players may gradually discover connections between their ancient identity and their existence in future civilizations.

Potential gameplay concepts include:

Consciousness transfer across eras.
Preservation of ancestral knowledge.
Royal bloodlines and spiritual inheritance.
Hidden pyramid chambers and ancient discoveries.
Recoverable artifacts, wealth and historical records.
Ancient Egyptian-inspired kingdoms connected to futuristic space civilizations.
The Duat as a transitional realm between worlds.

Historical Foundation

Real Egyptian history, architecture, religious beliefs and funerary practices will provide research inspiration. Supernatural powers, time travel and consciousness-transfer mechanisms are fictional interpretations developed specifically for the game.

# Itanimulli Legacy

Build the web companion hub and pitch site for "It's Anomaly" (also styled I.T.M.U.L.L.I.), an original open-world game concept that begins in a fictional ancient-Egypt-inspired kingdom and later continues in a far-future space era. This web app is the game's hub and pitch tool, not the full game. Label every system as Concept or Demo; nothing is a live game.

Visual style: cinematic and premium. Deep black and desert night tones, heavy royal gold, warm sand, Nile blue, subtle constellations and hieroglyph-style patterns (original symbols, not real hieroglyph text). Large dramatic headings.

Pages:
1. Home / Pitch: hero with the tagline "From street vendor to legend. Your legacy never dies." Sections for the world, routes to power, legacy across eras, and the space era.
2. The World: farm outskirts, city gate, market district, palace, temples, tombs, pyramids, the Nile. Card per location.
3. Routes to Power: Marketplace Hustler, Producer/Merchant, Military Leader, Political Operator, King or Queen, Mystic. Plus the ladder: Vendor, Fixer, Patron, Vizier, Pharaoh.
4. Soul & Legacy: Ka (life force) and Ba (reputation that travels across eras), the Weighing of the Heart judgment on death, succession through heirs or trusted allies, sealing curses and legendary return.
5. Legacy Codex (demo with sample data): a character's lineage, tomb record, and artifacts. Each artifact has a Scarab Seal ID with maker, materials, owners, and history.
6. The Duat (space era): a space-jail ship. Character creation happens during intake as a booking-photo screen where the player adjusts face, hair, skin tone and build before the final photo. Make a stylized demo booking-photo creator with sliders and a framed mugshot card.
7. Prototype: a simple top-down browser demo of one route, outer farm to city gate to market contact, with walking and one delivery objective.

Start with the Home pitch page, navigation, and the booking-photo creator.
